About Us

Kaavya Shree Research and Fashion Tech Private Limited is a research-driven firm committed to the improvement of innovation in interior fashion, textile materials, sustainable design technologies and integrated interior solutions.

Based on our concept, "Sharp | Deep | Broader | Solutions", we combine scientific research, innovative design thinking and technological excellence to produce products and solutions that respond to the evolving demands of modern living and commercial environments.

We explore advanced textile materials, performance textiles, eco-friendly techniques and intelligent design solutions that enhance usefulness, aesthetics and sustainability. We are committed to bridging the gap between traditional craftsmanship and modern technologies through ongoing research and development, generating creative solutions that are both practical and future-proof.

At Kaavya Shree Research and Fashion Tech Private Limited, we believe in sustainability. We are dedicated to encouraging ethical material choices, resource-efficient manufacturing processes and environmentally sound design approaches.

We integrate sustainable technologies with interior and fashion applications to help create healthier, more resilient and aesthetically inspiring spaces.

Our diversified know-how enables us to operate in a broad range of sectors including residential interiors, hospitality, commercial spaces, lifestyle products and specialised textile applications.

We partner with industry, research, design, manufacturing and institutional organisations to translate ideas into impactful solutions for real-world challenges.

Driven by curiosity, innovation and a commitment to excellence, Kaavya Shree Research and Fashion Tech Private Limited aims to be a leading force in shaping the future of interior fashion and sustainable design technology.

We believe meaningful research, creative vision and technological advancement can together create smarter materials, better environments and lasting value for businesses, communities and society.
